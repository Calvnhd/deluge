## Australia

### Nature and ambience

- [Australian Acoustic Observatory](https://data.acousticobservatory.org/) offers long-form environmental recordings from habitats across Australia, including birds, insects, weather and day-to-night soundscapes.
  - Licensing: [CC BY 4.0](https://acousticobservatory.org/data/find-data/) with attribution to the dataset and contributors.
- [EcoSounds](https://www.ecosounds.org/) hosts environmental recording projects with Australian wildlife calls, habitat ambiences and extended field recordings.
  - Licensing: Check each project's license badge and access conditions; some datasets are noncommercial-only or restricted.
- [ZapSplat](https://www.zapsplat.com/) offers Australian bush ambiences, cicadas and birds alongside a wider collection of environmental sounds and sound effects.
  - Licensing: [Custom license](https://www.zapsplat.com/license-type/standard-license/), not CC; a free account provides MP3 downloads with attribution, but standalone sample redistribution is prohibited.

### Historical speech and oral history

- [National Library of Australia oral history and folklore](https://www.library.gov.au/research/access-collection/using-oral-history-collection) provides interviews, regional voices, personal recollections, folklore and early radio recordings.
  - Licensing: [Conditions vary by interview](https://www.library.gov.au/services/copyright-library-collections/rights-and-oral-history-and-folklore-collection); check public-use permissions and notify the Library before publishing.
- [National Film and Sound Archive](https://www.nfsa.gov.au/collection) preserves Australian speeches, radio, advertisements, interviews, newsreels and other historical audiovisual recordings.
  - Licensing: [Rights clearance](https://www.nfsa.gov.au/industry/rights-usage) is usually needed from the relevant owners; supply fees may apply.
- [State Library of NSW oral history and sound](https://guides.sl.nsw.gov.au/oral-history-sound) collects local voices, migration stories, radio interviews, talks, performances and recollections of life in New South Wales.
  - Licensing: [Check the collection item's rights](https://www.sl.nsw.gov.au/copyright); the website's general CC license does not automatically cover archived recordings.
- [State Library of Western Australia oral histories](https://slwa.wa.gov.au/collections/collections/oral-histories) preserves voices describing mining, farming, migration, war and everyday life across Western Australia.
  - Licensing: [Copyright and donor conditions apply](https://slwa.wa.gov.au/about/corporate-information/copyright-library-collections); obtain permission where required before sampling.
- [State Library Victoria oral histories](https://www.slv.vic.gov.au/search-discover/explore-collections-format/audiovisual-material/oral-histories) includes interviews, speeches, broadcasts, folklore, working-life stories and recordings of writers and performers.
  - Licensing: [Check copyright and access restrictions](https://www.slv.vic.gov.au/help/copyright-legal-deposit/understanding-copyright/copying-re-use-without-librarys-permission); online listening does not itself permit reuse.
- [State Library of Queensland One Search](https://onesearch.slq.qld.gov.au/) provides access to Queensland oral histories, community voices and recordings of regional and wartime experiences.
  - Licensing: [Check each recording's rights](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/understanding-copyright) and request permission where needed; the website's CC license for text does not cover all audio.

## New Zealand

### Nature and ambience

- [Department of Conservation bird sounds](https://www.doc.govt.nz/nature/native-animals/birds/bird-songs-and-calls/) offers downloadable native bird calls, dawn choruses and bush recordings with surrounding wildlife and environmental sound.
  - Licensing: [CC BY 4.0](https://www.doc.govt.nz/footer-links/copyright/) permits commercial reuse with credit to the Department of Conservation (NZ); check any third-party exceptions.

### Historical speech and oral history

- [National Library of New Zealand oral history and sound](https://natlib.govt.nz/collections/a-z/oral-history-and-sound) preserves interviews, local voices, recollections, music and other recordings documenting New Zealand life.
  - Licensing: Check the collection's access conditions and discuss copying or reuse permission with Library staff; excerpts may be supplied following clearance.
- [Nga Taonga Sound & Vision](https://www.ngataonga.org.nz/search-use-collection/search/) holds historical radio, speeches, interviews, advertisements and film soundtracks from across New Zealand.
  - Licensing: [Rights and cultural clearances](https://www.ngataonga.org.nz/search-use-collection/rights-requesting/) are required as applicable; online listening is not a reuse license and supply fees may apply.
- [Auckland Libraries Kura Heritage Collections](https://kura.aucklandlibraries.govt.nz/) includes oral histories, community memories and local voices documenting Auckland and its people.
  - Licensing: Check each item's conditions and [contact the Library about reuse](https://kura.aucklandlibraries.govt.nz/digital/custom/help), especially for public or commercial sampling.

## International and multi-region

- [Citizen DJ, Library of Congress](https://citizen-dj.labs.loc.gov/) offers historical speech, interviews, dialects, music and found sounds selected for remixing.
  - Licensing: [Free to remix](https://citizen-dj.labs.loc.gov/about/); check each collection's reuse notes.
- [KB6 Drum Samples Archive](https://samples.kb6.de/) collects vintage drum-machine hits, electronic percussion and synth-derived drum sounds.
  - Licensing: [Custom terms](https://samples.kb6.de/faq.htm) distinguish music-making from sample redistribution; check the pack's included license, which takes precedence.
- [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) hosts community recordings of voices, machinery, household objects, wildlife, weather and ambient environments.
  - Licensing: Varies by sound, including CC0, CC BY and CC BY-NC; check the recording and [licensing FAQ](https://freesound.org/help/faq/#licenses).
- [National Park Service audio gallery](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/gallery.htm) collects wildlife, water, wind, insects, geothermal activity and human-made sounds from US national parks.
  - Licensing: The gallery states its clips are public domain and requests credit.
- [LibriVox](https://librivox.org/) offers volunteer-read books, poetry, dramatic readings and nonfiction in many languages.
  - Licensing: [Public domain in the US](https://librivox.org/pages/public-domain/); check the underlying work's copyright status in your country.
- [Wikimedia Commons audio](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Audio_files) includes animal calls, environmental recordings, pronunciation, music and historical speech.
  - Licensing: Check each file's license or public-domain statement; [reuse guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia) explains attribution and share-alike requirements.
- [BigSoundBank](https://bigsoundbank.com/) offers sound effects and field recordings of animals, everyday objects, transport and natural environments.
  - Licensing: [CC0](https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html) for sounds marked free and royalty-free; credit is appreciated, not required.
- [Luftrum free field recordings](https://www.luftrum.com/free-field-recordings/) offers ocean waves, forests, cityscapes, rain, room tones and recordings made with contact microphones and hydrophones.
  - Licensing: The free recordings use [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), requiring credit; the paid library has separate terms.
- [Xeno-canto](https://xeno-canto.org/) hosts wildlife recordings from around the world, including bird songs, calls and surrounding soundscapes.
  - Licensing: [Per-recording CC licenses](https://xeno-canto.org/about/terms); check noncommercial, no-derivatives and share-alike restrictions before sampling.
- [Internet Archive audio](https://archive.org/details/audio) contains archival speech, radio programmes, music, field recordings and space-mission communications.
  - Licensing: [Rights vary by item](https://help.archive.org/help/rights/); verify the stated license and provenance rather than assuming downloads are cleared for reuse.
- [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/) contains spoken word, performances, field recordings and sound effects alongside video.
  - Licensing: Use the Creative Commons search filter and verify the upload's [CC BY license](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2797468?hl=en); downloads must also comply with [YouTube's terms](https://www.youtube.com/static?template=terms).
- [Macaulay Library audio](https://search.macaulaylibrary.org/catalog?mediaType=audio) offers bird calls, songs and other wildlife recordings with species and location information.
  - Licensing: [Some contributors opt into CC](https://ebird.org/news/creative-commons-license-for-ebird-media), not the whole library; check the item and [request file access](https://support.ebird.org/en/support/solutions/articles/48001064551) when needed.
